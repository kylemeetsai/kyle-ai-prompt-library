CLAUDE BUILDS YOUR BUDGET 📊
One prompt · a live Google Sheet
Paste this prompt into Claude with Google Drive connected, fill in your own numbers, and Claude builds a real budget inside your Google Sheets. You get a phone-friendly dashboard with summary cards, color-coded progress bars, a 50/30/20 check and charts, plus a transaction log. Every number is a live formula, so when you log a purchase the whole dashboard updates by itself.
How to use
1. In Claude, go to Settings → Connectors and connect Google Drive
2. Tap Copy prompt below
3. Paste it into a new chat and replace the [brackets] with your own pay, categories and goals
4. Claude builds the sheet in your Drive and sends you the link. Log purchases in the Transactions tab and watch the Dashboard update
Create a Google Sheet in my Drive called "My Monthly Budget". Make it look like a polished personal-finance app, not a plain spreadsheet. ABOUT ME - My monthly take-home pay: [YOUR MONTHLY TAKE-HOME PAY] - My categories and monthly budgets: [e.g. Rent 1500, Utilities 150, Groceries 400, Gas 150, Eating Out 200, Subscriptions 50, Shopping 150, Fun Money 100, Emergency Fund 300] - My savings goals: [e.g. Emergency Fund $5,000 (saved $1,200 so far), Vacation $2,000 (saved $300)] If I left anything blank or it doesn't add up to my income, ask me before you build it. TAB 1: "Dashboard" (the first tab, built to read on a phone: keep it within columns A–D) - A title bar: "Monthly Budget" and the current month. - Big summary cards at the top: Income, Budgeted, Spent so far, Left to spend, and Savings rate (%). All formulas pulling from the other tabs. - A "Days left in month" cell, and "Safe to spend per day" = Left to spend divided by days left. - A category table: Category, Budget, Spent, Left, and a progress bar for each category using SPARKLINE (green under 80% used, amber 80–100%, red over 100%). - Group my categories into Needs, Wants and Savings, with a 50/30/20 check that shows each group's share of income next to its target. - A donut chart of spending by category and a bar chart of Budget vs Spent. TAB 2: "Transactions" - Columns: Date, Description, Category, Amount. - Category is a dropdown limited to my budget categories. - Leave it empty with plenty of rows so I can start logging. TAB 3: "Budget" - My categories and monthly budgets from above. - "Spent" for each category is a SUMIF over the Transactions tab, so logging a purchase updates everything automatically. TAB 4: "Goals" - My savings goals with saved so far, target, % done, a SPARKLINE progress bar, and the months left at the current monthly contribution. STYLE - One consistent palette: dark navy header bars, white text, soft gray card backgrounds, green for good, amber for close, red for over. - Currency formatting everywhere, frozen header rows, generous row heights, no gridlines on the Dashboard, a clean modern font. - Every number that can be a formula must be a formula, so the sheet stays live as I add transactions. When it's done, give me the link.
