=== description ===
Atlas, from World Labs, turns one photo into a 3D world you can fly a camera through, even the parts the camera never saw. These are my 3 rules for photos that build great worlds, plus the steps to walk inside yours.

=== steps ===
Tap <em>Copy rules</em> below and keep them in your notes
Take your photo (or 3) following the rules, or pick a wide, sharp old one
Go to <strong>atlas.worldlabs.ai</strong>, sign in, open <em>Viewfinder</em>, and follow the steps in the rules

=== notes ===
Good to know: some steps use paid credits, so check the cost in the app before you click. On a free account, World Labs can use your photos to train its AI, so leave out anything private.

=== prompt ===
My 3 photo rules for Atlas (by World Labs):
1. Go wide. Stand in a corner (or at the edge of the space) and shoot across it, phone sideways, at chest height.
2. Bright, sharp, empty. Lights on, phone steady, floor and a bit of ceiling in the shot, no people or pets.
3. Want it closer to the real place? Add 2 more photos. Step a few feet to the side for each one, keep about half of the last shot in view, same lights, nothing moved.

How to walk inside your photo:
1. Go to atlas.worldlabs.ai, sign in, open Viewfinder.
2. Upload one photo, or your 3 together. Using an old pic? Pick a wide, sharp one where people aren't the main thing.
3. Let it build, then drag the camera around. Anything your photo didn't show is the AI's best guess.
4. Draw a camera path and hit Render to get a video.
5. No photo? Viewfinder can make one for you. Try: "a cozy reading nook in a treehouse at golden hour, wide angle, eye level, warm lamps".

Heads up: some steps use paid credits, so check the cost in the app before you click. On a free account, World Labs can use your photos to train its AI, so leave out anything private.
